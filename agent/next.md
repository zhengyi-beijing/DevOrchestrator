# P20.3 — Deterministic Recovery & Heterogeneous Escalation

Status: **READY_TO_RUN**

Successor: P20.4

## Objective and frozen direction

Implement the pure bounded Recovery Policy in `agent/staged/P20.md` and
`docs/P20_OWNER_REQUIREMENTS.md` section 5. Decisions cover all eight required
actions; existing lifecycle/coordinators retain ownership and final safety
fences. Persist reservations/attempts/strategy/outcome before and after effects.
Replay reconciles existing effect IDs or owner-gates crash ambiguity.

Same strategy retry at most once, at most three recoveries per incident;
account rotation alone cannot bypass repetition. Require changed context,
strategy, model, provider or repair plan, with available eligible heterogeneous
escalation for semantic/high-risk/repeated failures. Preserve provider-level
reviewer independence. Unknown safety, stale HEAD, unsafe dirt, conflicting
authority or exhausted budget becomes durable actionable stable OWNER_GATE.

## Approved executable design

Converge the recovery rules currently scattered across Watchdog, AGY routing, planner/reviewer retry and provider handling into one pure, bounded, deterministic Recovery Policy plus a durable recovery-attempt ledger, and close the three confirmed bootstrap recovery gaps. A new pure module `src/dev_orchestrator/incidents/recovery_policy.py` consumes current lifecycle authority, the incident/failure family, durable previous attempts, real resource availability and finite policy budgets, and returns exactly one of RETRY_SAME_STRATEGY, RETRY_CHANGED_STRATEGY, SWITCH_RESOURCE, HETEROGENEOUS_ESCALATION, RESTART_COMPONENT, RECONCILE_RUNTIME, OWNER_GATE, NO_ACTION_ALREADY_RECOVERED with reason, authorized effect, finite remaining budget and exact expected authority/source anchors. It is not a new state machine and holds no authority: TransitionExecutor, WatchdogCoordinator, PlanCoordinator, JobRecovery and OwnerControlStore remain the sole owners and re-enforce owner/clean-Git/idempotency/role-independence fences immediately before every effect. A companion `recovery_ledger.py` persists attempt reservations before effects and outcomes after them inside the existing IncidentStore journal/lock, so restart remembers attempts, strategies, repeats, escalation and gates; replay reconciles exactly one existing effect identity or fails closed to a durable actionable OWNER_GATE. Same-strategy retries default to at most one, total recoveries per incident to at most three, AGY account rotation alone never qualifies as a changed strategy, and semantic/high-risk/repeated failures require an actually configured eligible heterogeneous resource or gate. Integration work carries the semantic remediation/rejection budget across review-resource recovery and restart, replaces the silent failed-plan stall after transport response loss with exact-request bounded reconciliation or a gate, and bounds role-tool child/harness cleanup without ever retrying an ambiguously live Worker. Behaviour is verified against every registered control-plane fault scenario CPF-01, CPF-02, CPF-03, CPF-04, CPF-05, CPF-06, CPF-07, CPF-08, CPF-09 and CPF-10.

### Validation plan
- RED-first evidence: `docs/evidence/P20_3_RED.txt` holds the real, unedited failing output of the new focused suites run against canonical pre-change source, with explicit failure counts.
- Deterministic purity and decision matrix: for each of the eight actions, a table-driven test asserts the exact action, reason, authorized effect, anchors and remaining budget, asserts byte-identical `to_dict()` on repeated calls with identical inputs and an injected `now`, and asserts `decide_recovery` performs no file, process, git, network or ambient-clock access.
- Anti-loop budgets and rotation: same strategy retried at most once; at most three total recoveries per incident; the fourth request returns OWNER_GATE with a finite exhausted budget and an exact owner action. Two attempts differing only by AGY account share one `strategy_key`, consume the same budget, and the repeat yields HETEROGENEOUS_ESCALATION or OWNER_GATE rather than another rotation.
- Resource decisions: with an eligible configured heterogeneous resource the decision is HETEROGENEOUS_ESCALATION naming that exact resource, with none it is OWNER_GATE, and no unconfigured model or provider ever appears; provider-level reviewer independence survives escalation and a candidate that would collapse it gates instead; `provider_quota_exhausted`, `provider_unavailable`, `aibroker_unavailable` and `transport_unavailable` each yield SWITCH_RESOURCE with exact resource exclusion within budget, then OWNER_GATE on exhaustion.
- Reviewer reject: a `reviewer_rejected` incident drives bounded semantic remediation, not resource rotation, and consumes the semantic budget exactly once per real rejection.
- Safety gates: `stale_head`, `unexpected_dirty_worktree`, contradictory lineage, unknown safety and conflicting authority each return OWNER_GATE once with no actuation and no repository mutation. This includes CPF-04 (contradictory or ambiguous successor claims fail closed with no transition created) and CPF-05 (dirty worktree defers repair, waits without mutation, and advances only once clean without an owner continue).
- CPF-01: a stale predecessor Worker running after successor publication keeps the source authoritative and fences the target; the recovery policy returns RECONCILE_RUNTIME or OWNER_GATE and `resolve_role_liveness` returning `alive is None` never produces a retry, restart or cleanup decision for a Worker. A proven-dead `worker_process_lost` reconciles the existing execution identity instead of launching a new one.
- CPF-02: an accepted NEXT_TASK review decision whose handoff is missing converges through watchdog_recovery to exactly one rebuilt handoff; the recovery ledger reserves one attempt, repeated ticks reuse that attempt_id, and no second handoff or duplicate control command is produced.
- CPF-03: a daemon restart mid-transition replays the missing handoff from the transition journal; `reconcile_recovery_attempts` binds the reserved attempt to that replayed effect, creates no parallel handoff, and leaves SUCCESSOR_HANDOFF_LINEAGE_VALID intact.
- CPF-06 and CPF-10: repeated daemon ticks over unchanged state create no duplicate transitions, no generation increment, no new ledger records and no store revision; same-HEAD repeated launch retries against an un-repaired declaration gate stay byte-stable with no timestamp refresh in either the gate or the attempt record.
- CPF-07: a crash after transition intent but before publication converges to a single authoritative owner; recovery reconciliation is run repeatedly and still yields exactly one live handoff and one terminal attempt record.
- CPF-08: a control-plane task missing its declaration fails closed to the CONTROL_PLANE_DECLARATION_REQUIRED owner gate at worker_launch; the recovery policy returns OWNER_GATE, never RESTART_COMPONENT or a retry, and emits no actuation.

### Risks / failure modes
- Scope creep into P20.4: the eight actions, the ledger, the CPF-01..CPF-10 conformance tests and the three bootstrap gap closures are the whole task. No fault-injection harness, soak supervisor, isolated template repo or live qualification run belongs in P20.3.
- Planner control-flow edits are the highest-risk change: `core/ai_planner.py` is ~2.8k lines and its reviewer failover/remediation/recovery interaction is subtle. Carrying the semantic budget could regress the eight accepted bootstrap contract tests or the plan remediation/hard-reject bounds. Keep edits surgical, additive and covered by RED tests before touching the loop.
- Signature or family drift would break P16.10, P20.1 and P20.2 acceptance. Strategy keys and ledger fields must stay outside `ALLOWED_FINGERPRINT_FIELDS`, exactly as P20.2 handled `taxonomy_class`.
- Touching the recovery path can regress the existing CPF matrix: CPF-02, CPF-03 and CPF-07 all assert single-owner convergence over handoffs that the new ledger now also records. Reserve attempts around the existing recovery effect rather than issuing a parallel one, and run `test_p1613_successor_consistency.py` and `test_p1614_invariant_workflow.py` on every iteration.
- A new gate-emission path could accidentally auto-clear or re-open a genuine owner gate, spam duplicates, or refresh a timestamp and break the CPF-10 byte-stability assertion. Gates must be emitted once per attempt record and resolvable only through existing authority.
- Double actuation is the core durability hazard: if a reservation is written outside the store lock, or dedup is decided against a stale pre-transaction snapshot, two Workers or jobs could start. Every reservation must be decided inside `execute_txn` under the inter-process lock.
- Flipping telemetry coverage from PARTIAL could turn unknown coverage into a misleading numeric zero. Coverage must stay `None` with an explicit reason whenever the ledger cannot answer.
- Determinism depends on injected time and source-event identities. Any residual use of wall clock, daemon tick counters or process-salted `hash()` in attempt IDs would break restart idempotence and the CPF-06 byte-stable expectation — the exact defect P20.2 remediated in `_detect_execution_transport_failure`.
- Windows-specific durability: inter-process file locking, long paths and atomic replace under the shared `runtime/` root. Reuse the existing `InterProcessFileLock` and `json_store` write path rather than new IO helpers; `test_json_store_windows.py` must stay green.
- The inconclusive-reconciliation gate changes a currently terminal `failed` outcome into a gate. If scoped too broadly it could gate on ordinary semantic failures; the condition must be narrow (transport/response loss with the Broker still running and no terminal provider status) and asserted negatively for semantic and cancelled/no_candidate statuses.
- Bounded child-process cleanup touches process termination. Over-broad matching could kill the daemon or a user process; it must require verified exact process identity plus source/resource correlation, as the bootstrap repair did.
- The pure policy overlaps conceptually with the existing P17 `convergence.decide` ladder. Duplicating its logic risks divergent behaviour; the plan reuses its patterns and effect-reconciliation helpers rather than reimplementing a second decision engine, and must not change P17 semantics or its replay determinism corpus.
- Full `tests_py` is ~1700 tests and roughly 12 minutes; the required adjacent groups are large. Budget verification time accordingly and run focused GREEN before regression, rather than discovering a broad regression late.
