# P16.10 Automatic Failure Harvesting & Regression Promotion

Status: **PENDING DESIGN**

## Goal

Make DevOrchestrator learn new orchestration failure modes from its own runtime evidence. Repeated owner control actions such as continue, retry, reconcile, daemon restart, or manual recovery become incident evidence instead of disappearing as operator actions.

Target pipeline:
runtime anomaly -> incident capture -> fingerprint/dedup -> candidate regression -> deterministic reproduction -> independent validation -> promoted regression.

This milestone does not authorize self-modifying production code.

## Core invariant

Human intervention is an incident signal whenever the active ExecutionIntent should have continued without new owner information.

If a control-only owner action changes a project from unexplained no-progress to forward progress, with no genuine OWNER_GATE, explicit stop, safety gate, or new information, DevO must record a continuation-gap incident automatically.

Every non-terminal lifecycle state must have exactly one of: executable successor; declared external wait with observable completion; genuine owner gate; terminal outcome; or deterministic recoverable-error path.

A stable state outside those categories is an orchestration fault.

## 1. Automatic incident capture

Capture incidents without requiring a chat instruction when:
- active ExecutionIntent plus non-null next_action remains stably IDLE beyond its daemon-tick budget;
- a durable AI result exists but is not consumed into a lifecycle effect or explicit blocker;
- watchdog performs or requests recovery;
- an accepted/running execution vanishes, stalls, or violates its terminal contract;
- daemon restart/reconcile changes outcome for the same durable identity;
- owner continue/retry/reconcile/restart causes immediate progress after unexplained no-progress;
- a short lifecycle cycle or repeated identical recovery fingerprint exhausts its bounded budget.

Incident capture must be append-only, idempotent and restart-safe.
## 2. Incident evidence packet

Persist a bounded machine-readable packet containing at least:
- incident_id, schema_version, created_at, project_id, task_id and classification;
- immutable Git/head/recovery-epoch/control identities;
- lifecycle state before and after the incident;
- active ExecutionIntent/ExecutionContext references, next_action and progress obligation;
- Worker/Reviewer/Planner/Broker states and durable execution/review-decision references;
- blocker codes, watchdog diagnosis and recovery fingerprint;
- owner action, if any, and whether it supplied new information or merely retriggered progress;
- evidence references to authoritative ledgers/status snapshots;
- bounded timestamps sufficient to reconstruct the transition sequence.

Do not persist hidden model chain-of-thought. Store only auditable conclusions, structured state and evidence references. Observation, inference and unknown evidence must remain distinct.

## 3. Fingerprint and deduplication

Generate a stable failure fingerprint from semantic state rather than volatile IDs. Include at least failure class/blocker code, lifecycle stage, project/task contract class, Worker/Reviewer/Broker state class, Git-anchor relation, recovery/continuation relation and invariant identifier.

Normalize timestamps, request UUIDs and incidental values out of the dedup key. Repeated occurrences increment recurrence count and attach evidence to the existing incident family.

## 4. Candidate regression generation

For every new incident fingerprint, automatically create a regression candidate defining:
- minimal preconditions;
- triggering event sequence;
- expected lifecycle invariant/transition;
- observed invalid outcome;
- proposed deterministic fixture;
- required oracle;
- source incident/evidence references.

An AI role may synthesize a candidate test, but its output is advisory evidence only. Prefer synthetic/local fixtures over live Web, RDC, SSH, provider or hardware dependencies.
## 5. Regression promotion gate

A candidate enters the permanent suite only when all applicable gates pass:
1. Reproduction: deterministically reproduces the incident or semantic equivalent on a known failing anchor/fixture.
2. Discrimination: passes on a known corrected anchor/fixture or after bounded remediation.
3. Stability: repeated executions produce the same classification and oracle.
4. Isolation: no real X-ray, conveyor/VFD, destructive Git, live browser state, or uncontrolled external service.
5. Deduplication: no existing regression already covers the same invariant/fingerprint.
6. Review: an independent Reviewer accepts the invariant, fixture and oracle.

Failed promotion remains a candidate with an explicit reason; it is not silently discarded.

## 6. Owner-intervention attribution

Classify owner actions as NEW_INFORMATION, CONTROL_ONLY, or EXPLICIT_STOP.

CONTROL_ONLY includes continue/retry/reconcile/restart/refresh that supplies no new technical information. When CONTROL_ONLY restores progress, treat that as strong evidence for AUTO_CONTINUATION_GAP or a more specific failure class.

Preserve before/after state and determine whether the same transition should have occurred automatically under the existing ExecutionIntent. Genuine OWNER_GATE decisions are not orchestration defects.

## 7. Progress obligations

Introduce or reuse a machine-readable ProgressObligation for each waiting/running state:
- expected_event or successor_action;
- evidence source;
- bounded deadline/tick budget where appropriate;
- timeout classification;
- permitted automatic recovery;
- stop/escalation condition.

Examples: REVIEWING -> reviewer terminal result; REMEDIATE_READY -> remediation Worker launch on next eligible tick; NEXT_TASK_READY -> lifecycle transition on next eligible tick; EXECUTING -> Worker terminal/reconciliation evidence.

This contract distinguishes legal waiting from silent deadlock.
## 8. Automated triage and metrics

Expose incident families and candidate status through existing API/UI/mobile observability surfaces.

Track manual CONTROL_ONLY interventions per task, continuation-gap incidents, unique versus recurring fingerprints, candidates generated, promotion/pass/reject counts, false-positive/reclassified incidents, and mean time from incident to deterministic reproduction.

Representative unattended-run success requires CONTROL_ONLY intervention count = 0 when all required information was already available.

## Acceptance

Build deterministic acceptance covering:
1. Persisted REMEDIATE verdict remains unconsumed while daemon is alive.
2. No true owner gate exists and ExecutionIntent remains active.
3. A synthetic owner continue causes the remediation Worker to launch.
4. DevO records the before/after incident and classifies the owner action as CONTROL_ONLY.
5. A stable fingerprint is produced and one regression candidate is created.
6. Repeating the incident attaches recurrence evidence instead of duplicating the candidate.
7. Candidate generation creates a deterministic fixture/test proposal.
8. Promotion proves fail-before/pass-after and repeated stability before admission.
9. Genuine OWNER_GATE plus owner decision is not classified as a continuation defect.
10. Daemon restart during capture/candidate/promotion loses or duplicates no state.
11. Healthy daemon ticks without task evidence never mask a stalled task; `SYSTEM_ALIVE`, `TASK_ACTIVE`, and `TASK_PROGRESSING` are independently observable.
12. A `REVIEW_FAILED`/`OWNER_GATE` task with no live role process and stale task evidence is classified `ORCHESTRATOR_ALIVE_TASK_STALLED`; bounded recovery is attempted or an explicit unrecoverable reason is surfaced.

Also use representative P16.7, P16.8 and P16.9 incidents as seed cases for the harvesting pipeline.

## Safety and non-goals

- Do not automatically merge production fixes solely because a generated test passes.
- Do not weaken owner, Git/head, security or physical-safety gates.
- Do not auto-start X-ray sources, conveyors/VFDs or other physical hardware.
- Do not let candidate tests mutate production repositories or external services.
- Do not persist hidden model reasoning.
- Do not equate every timeout or owner action with a bug; evidence must satisfy the invariant classifier.

## Relationship to P16.7-P16.9

P16.7 establishes self-healing activation/readiness and structured blockers.
P16.8 establishes persistent ExecutionIntent/ExecutionContext and golden-path liveness.
P16.9 closes execution-loss/watchdog lineage gaps.
P16.10 turns failures of those invariants into automatically harvested, deduplicated and promotable regression knowledge so future continuation defects no longer depend on the owner noticing and describing each case manually.
