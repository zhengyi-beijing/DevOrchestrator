# P15 Mobile Observability & Guarded Control

Status: **PENDING DESIGN**

Goal: provide an Android-native, failure-independent observation and bounded-control client without creating a second lifecycle authority.

Scope:
- Connect Android directly to DevO over Tailscale and consume authoritative lifecycle, durable-job and normalized review/finding state.
- Show project/task/stage, active role/provider, current/next action, job progress, freshness, watchdog/diagnostics and OWNER_GATE.
- Provide reconnectable event updates and guarded continue/pause/resume/stop/retry/reconcile/OWNER_GATE controls through DevO authority.
- Raise deduplicated vibration/optional sound alerts for confirmed no-progress and keep disconnected/unknown distinct.
- Support per-project/global alert policy, thresholds, quiet hours, acknowledgement and snooze.

Acceptance:
- With ChatGPT/browser/RDC unavailable, Android can observe authoritative execution, distinguish running/stalled/disconnected states, receive alerts and execute supported guarded controls.

## Independent plan-review constraints carried forward

The first independent Plan Reviewer rejected the initial P15 design on two blocking boundaries that must remain explicit in every recovered/revised plan:

- **Single revocation authority:** mobile-device authentication and revocation must have one canonical source of truth. The executable design must name that authority and define fail-closed behavior across pairing, registration, validation, revocation, crash recovery, and any partial-write condition so a revoked token cannot remain usable because two stores disagree.
- **Alert thresholds are notification policy only:** mobile global/per-project thresholds may control notification/suppression/escalation behavior, but they must not independently classify authoritative lifecycle progress. Running/unknown/watchdog state cannot be promoted to stalled/no-progress by the mobile layer; the design and acceptance tests must make that separation explicit.

Design note: detailed executable design must be independently reviewed before implementation.
